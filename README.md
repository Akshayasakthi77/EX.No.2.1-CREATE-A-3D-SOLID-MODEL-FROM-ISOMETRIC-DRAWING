# EX.No.2.1  CREATE A 3D SOLID MODEL FROM ISOMETRIC DRAWING
## Date:12-10-2026

## AIM
To create a 3Dmodel from the given isometric drawing using fusion 360 tool. 

## REQUIREMENTS
1. Autodesk fusion 360
2. Windows 10
3. 16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 1 GB or more)
4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload 

## PROCEDURE

 ### STEP-1: Step 1: Base Body (Main Camera Box)
 Create Sketch: Select Create Sketch and choose the Front (XZ) plane.

 Draw Rectangle: Choose Center Rectangle, click the origin, and dimension it 100 mm width by 50 mm height. Finish sketch.
 Extrude: Select Extrude, click the rectangle, type 35 mm for the depth, and press Enter.

 ### STEP-2:   Main Lens (Front Cylinder)Create Sketch: 
 Select Create Sketch and click on the front surface of the main rectangular block.
 Draw Circle: Choose Center Circle at the origin, type a diameter of $\phi 50\text{ mm}$, and finish the sketch.
 Extrude: Select Extrude, click the circle, type 20 mm for the extrusion distance, and press Enter.
 <img width="492" height="561" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-05 101420" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/12619a74-f01b-4c4b-9d07-5ebbd1d9103b" />


### STEP-3:Central Top Prism/Flash Housing
Create Sketch: Create a sketch on the top surface of the main block.
Draw Profile: Draw a trapezoidal/rectangular profile centered on top, with a length of 30 mm across.
Extrude: Extrude the shape upward by 10 mm. (If adding the 45 degree angle cut sides, use the Chamber or Cut tool accordingly based on the drawing dimensions).


<img width="461" height="521" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-11 174747" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/54da7903-2956-417f-a4c0-d28fa71b855b" />

 ### STEP-4: Left Top Small Button/Block
 Create Sketch: Create a sketch on the top surface of the main block.
 Position & Draw: Draw a small square located 15 mm from the left edge and 15 mm from the back edge, with dimensions of  15 mm x 15 mm. Finish sketch.
 Extrude: Extrude this square upward by 5 mm.
 

 ### STEP-5:   Right Top Circular Button
 Create Sketch: Create a sketch on the top surface of the main block.
 Position & Draw: Draw a circle with a diameter of 20mm, positioned 15 mm from the right edge and 15 mm from the back edge. Finish sketch.Extrude: Extrude the circle upward by 10 mm.

### STEP-6: Click finish sketch.
